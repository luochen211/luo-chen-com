# Storyboard

## 01 — cover
Role: hook
Title: 个人网站，怎么做得炫酷？
Takeaway: 炫酷不是堆特效，而是设计信息如何被看见。
Visual: abstract dark browser frame with a scroll line and layered content cards.

## 02 — why
Title: 为什么还要做个人网站？
Takeaway: 把简历、作品、判断和联系方式放进一个自己能控制的公开资产。
Visual: scattered social cards converge into one owned home.

## 03 — reframe
Title: 炫酷，不是动得多
Takeaway: 视觉效果服务于注意力和理解。
Visual: left noisy motion field crossed out; right calm three-step attention path.

## 04 — scrollytelling
Title: 先让滚动变成叙事
Takeaway: Scroll position becomes a timeline for information.
Visual: vertical sequence: claim -> proof -> work -> contact.

## 05 — space
Title: 用空间制造“正在发生”
Takeaway: sticky, layers, depth and stacking make the page feel spatial.
Visual: fixed viewport with cards entering, overlapping, and rotating.

## 06 — rhythm
Title: 动效要有节奏，不要有噪音
Takeaway: reveal / hold / transition / payoff.
Visual: four-beat timeline with restrained motion cues.

## 07 — lettering
Title: 艺术字：先描述视觉，再反推提示词
Takeaway: prompt = 字体结构 + 材质 + 光线 + 构图 + 禁止项。
Visual: prompt fragments become a typographic mark.

## 08 — deployment
Title: 从本地到线上：Cloudflare Pages
Takeaway: Git push -> build -> Pages -> custom domain; 国内访问取决于域名、DNS、缓存和网络条件，不能把部署成功等同于全国可访问。
Visual: a deployment pipeline with a separate mainland-access checkpoint.

## 09 — case study
Title: 这个网站的滚动编排
Takeaway: hero → proof scenes → orbit projects → stacked writing → expanding CTA.
Visual: one long horizontal storyboard of five scenes.

## 10 — formula
Title: 一套可复用的炫酷公式
Takeaway: 内容骨架 × 空间关系 × 动效反馈 × 留白。
Visual: four interlocking modules around a central experience.

## 11 — closing
Title: 最好的炫酷，是让人愿意继续往下看
Takeaway: Design the next scroll, not just the first screen.
Visual: a single glowing scroll path moving into an open dark field.
